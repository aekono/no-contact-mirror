## Full Description

### Transform Your Healing Journey

No-Contact is a supportive companion app designed to help you maintain healthy boundaries and track your personal growth during a no-contact period. Whether you're healing from a toxic relationship, setting boundaries with family, or taking time for self-discovery, this app provides the tools and encouragement you need.

### Key Features

**📊 Daily Check-ins**
- Track your mood and emotional state
- Record personal thoughts and reflections
- Build a comprehensive journal of your journey

**📈 Progress Analytics**
- Visual mood trends over time
- Streak tracking and milestone celebrations
- Identify patterns and growth areas

**🎯 Personal Goals**
- Set meaningful milestones
- Track progress toward your objectives
- Celebrate achievements along the way

**💪 Motivational Support**
- Personalized reasons for your no-contact journey
- Encouraging quotes and reminders
- Gentle daily check-ins

**🔒 Complete Privacy**
- All data stored locally on your device
- No personal information transmitted
- Full control over your data

### Why Choose No-Contact?

**Privacy-First Design**
Your healing journey is personal. We believe your data should stay on your device, not on our servers. All your thoughts, feelings, and progress remain completely private.

**Evidence-Based Approach**
Track your emotional patterns, identify triggers, and celebrate genuine progress. Data-driven insights help you understand your healing process.

**Non-Judgmental Support**
Whether you're on day 1 or day 100, the app meets you where you are. No pressure, no judgment, just gentle encouragement.

**Beautiful, Intuitive Design**
Clean, calming interface designed to reduce stress, not add to it. Focus on your healing, not on learning a complicated app.
