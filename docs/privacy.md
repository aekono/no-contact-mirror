# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 2024**

## Overview

No-Contact is designed with privacy as a core principle. This app stores all your data locally on your device and does not transmit any personal information to external servers.

## Data Collection

### What We Collect
- **Nothing automatically** - All data stays on your device
- **No personal information** is transmitted to our servers
- **No analytics tracking** of your usage patterns
- **No third-party data sharing**

### What We Store Locally
- Your check-in entries (mood ratings and notes)
- Your personal reasons for no-contact
- Your goals and achievements
- App settings and preferences
- Streak counters and progress data

## Data Usage

### Local Storage Only
- All data is stored securely on your device using standard mobile storage
- Data is encrypted at rest using your device's built-in security
- You can export your data at any time through the app's export feature

### No External Transmission
- We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal data
- No data is sent to our servers or third-party services
- No usage analytics or crash reporting data is collected

## Your Control

### Data Management
- **Export**: You can export all your data as a JSON file
- **Delete**: You can delete individual entries or reset all data
- **Backup**: Use your device's backup system to preserve your data

### App Permissions
- **Notifications**: Used only for daily reminders (optional)
- **Storage**: Used to save your data locally on your device
- **No other permissions** are required

## Third-Party Services

### None
- This app does not use any third-party analytics services
- No advertising networks or tracking pixels
- No social media integrations that share data

## Data Security

### Local Security
- All data is stored using your device's secure storage
- Data is protected by your device's lock screen security
- No data is accessible to other apps without your permission

### No Server Storage
- We do not maintain any servers that store your data
- No risk of data breaches from our systems
- Your data never leaves your device unless you choose to export it

## Children's Privacy

This app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update this document. Since we don't collect data, changes will primarily affect how we handle any future features.

## Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can contact us through the app's feedback feature.

---

**Summary**: Your data stays on your device. We don't collect, store, or transmit any personal information. You're in complete control of your data.
